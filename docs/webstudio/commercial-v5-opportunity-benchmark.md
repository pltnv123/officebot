# Commercial V5 Opportunity Benchmark

**Purpose:** Market positioning and competitive analysis

## Market Opportunity

### Target Segments

| Segment | Characteristics | Budget Range |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| B2B Services | 10-200 employees, ops-focused | 8K-15K |
| Professional Services | Legal, consulting, high-trust | 10K-20K |
| E-commerce | Premium brands, refresh needed | 12K-25K |
| Healthcare/Wellness | Booking funnels, compliance | 10K-18K |
| Crypto/Finance | Urgent launches, premium | 15K-30K |
| Real Estate | Luxury, villas, high-value | 12K-25K |

### Total Addressable Market

- **Primary:** 500-1000 companies (regional)
- **Secondary:** 2000-5000 companies (expanded)
- **Conversion Target:** 2-4 clients/month
- **Revenue Potential:** 20K-60K/month at steady state

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. Freelancers

**Advantages:**
- QA scorecard system
- Delivery archives
- Post-launch roadmap
- Professional documentation

**Price Position:** Premium (2-3x freelancer rates)

### vs. Agencies

**Advantages:**
- Faster timeline (4-6 weeks vs 8-12)
- Transparent pricing bands
- Owner dependency clarity
- No retainer required

**Price Position:** Competitive (50-70% of agency rates)

### vs. DIY Builders

**Advantages:**
- Compliance-aware copy
- Professional QA
- Integration expertise
- Time savings (4 weeks vs 12+ weeks DIY)

**Price Position:** Premium but justified by ROI

## Value Proposition

### For Founders/Ops

- **Speed:** 4-6 weeks from asset receipt
- **Quality:** 87/100 average QA score
- **Clarity:** No surprises, documented dependencies
- **ROI:** Professional presence → higher conversion

### For Marketing Teams

- **Compliance:** Forbidden claims flagged
- **Accessibility:** Basic a11y checks
- **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first CSS
- **Integration:** Placeholder until credentials

## Pricing Strategy

### Bands

| Tier | Price | Target | Margin |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| Basic | 5K-8K | Landing pages | 60% |
| Standard | 8K-15K | Multi-section | 65% |
| Premium | 15K-25K | Complex integrations | 70% |
| Enterprise | 25K+ | Custom scope | 75% |

### Urgency Premium

- **Standard:** Baseline timeline
- **Urgent (<3 weeks):** +20%
- **Critical (<2 weeks):** +40%, case-by-case

## Risk Factors

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Client acquisition slow | Medium | High | Refine outreach, partnerships |
| Scope creep | Medium | Medium | Clear exclusions, change orders |
| Timeline delays | Low | Medium | Asset receipt gating |
| Compliance issues | Low | High | Assumptions documented, legal exclusion |

## Differentiation

1. **QA Scorecard** — Quantified quality, not subjective
2. **Delivery Archives** — Professional handoff, not "here's the files"
3. **Owner Dependencies** — Clear decision tracking, not surprises
4. **Post-Launch Roadmap** — Growth planning, not "good luck"
5. **Red Team Review** — Proactive risk identification
