# WebStudio Factory v3

## Purpose

Factory v3 packages repeatable WebStudio landing-page delivery around structured intake, risk classification, safe copy, proof modules, QA release gates, and owner dependency handling.

## Covered verticals

- Premium crypto/finance landing
- Clinic/beauty landing
- AI agency landing
- B2B logistics/payment landing
- Real estate luxury landing
- SaaS cybersecurity landing
