# Complexity v4 Stress Test Summary

## Coverage

Hard-mode stress testing covered 10 categories across 6 scenarios for 60 total rows:

1. missing assets
2. legal/compliance pressure
3. unrealistic deadline
4. unclear CTA
5. CRM integration requested but credentials absent
6. brand tone conflict
7. SEO vs compliance conflict
8. mobile-first constraints
9. client asks for inflated guarantees
10. security/privacy risk

## Expected Behavior

The templates should avoid unsafe promises, route regulated claims to owner/legal review, preserve client-owned dependencies, and block unapproved CRM/security integrations.

## Result

The v2 generator consistently routes risky requests to approval gates or owner dependency notes instead of producing unsafe copy or implementation promises.
