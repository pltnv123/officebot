# Sales Kit

## Purpose

Sales kit provides consistent messaging, objection handling, and pricing guidance for WebStudio engagements.

## Contents

- `/output/webstudio-sales-kit.md` — what we deliver, what requires approval, what we cannot promise
- `/output/webstudio-objection-handling-playbook.md` — responses to common objections
- `/output/webstudio-pricing-scope-matrix-v5.md` — budget bands and add-ons
- `/output/webstudio-discovery-call-script.md` — structured discovery questions
- `/output/webstudio-client-onboarding-checklist.md` — pre-kickoff through post-launch
- `/output/webstudio-client-risk-disclaimer-library.md` — industry-specific disclaimers

## Key Principles

- No fake guarantees
- Document owner dependencies visibly
- Compliance review for regulated industries
- Placeholders until credentials provided
- Separate deployment approval required
