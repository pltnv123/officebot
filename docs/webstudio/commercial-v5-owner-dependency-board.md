## Risk Mitigations

### Asset Delays

**Risk:** Client doesn't provide assets on time

**Mitigation:**
- Timeline explicitly starts from asset receipt
- Placeholder content offered (additional cost)
- Escalation at 3 days overdue

### Decision Paralysis

**Risk:** Owner can't decide on options

**Mitigation:**
- Limited choices (2-3 options max)
- Recommendation provided with each option
- Default path if no decision in 48 hours

### Third-Party Delays

**Risk:** External services slow to provision

**Mitigation:**
- Early account creation checklist
- Placeholder integrations in production
- Credentials required before go-live, not before production

### Compliance Uncertainty

**Risk:** Industry regulations unclear

**Mitigation:**
- Assumptions documented in proposal
- Legal review exclusion in scope
- Client confirms compliance responsibility

## Owner Commitment Levels

### Light Touch (Recommended)

- Weekly 15-min sync call
- 48-hour decision turnaround
- Asset delivery by Week 3
- One design revision round
- One content revision round

**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks

### High Involvement

- Bi-weekly 30-min sync calls
- 24-hour decision turnaround
- Asset delivery by Week 2
- Multiple revision rounds
- Hands-on content review

**Timeline:** 6-8 weeks

### Delegated

- Single point of contact (marketing lead)
- Weekly async updates
- Decision turnaround: 72 hours
- Asset delivery by Week 4

**Timeline:** 6-8 weeks

## Escalation Protocol

| Issue | Trigger | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Asset delay | 3 days overdue | Email reminder |
| Asset delay | 7 days overdue | Call + revised timeline |
| Decision delay | 48 hours overdue | Default path proposed |
| Decision delay | 5 days overdue | Project pause notice |
| Scope change | Any | Change order required |
| Timeline change | Any | Revised schedule + approval |
