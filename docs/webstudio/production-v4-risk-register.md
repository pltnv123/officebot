# WebStudio Production V4 Risk Register

## Risks

1. **Compliance risk** — regulated industries require owner legal review
2. **Asset risk** — missing brand assets block polish
3. **Integration risk** — credentials absent for live forms
4. **Timeline risk** — client may expect unrealistic turnaround
5. **Claims risk** — client may request forbidden guarantees

## Mitigations

- Placeholder policy applied
- Compliance-safe wording used
- Owner dependencies documented
- Client education templates planned

## Owner Decisions Required

1. Approve placeholder policy for missing assets
2. Provide integration credentials for live forms
3. Compliance/legal review for regulated industries
4. Approve deployment separately
