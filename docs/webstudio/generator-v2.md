# WebStudio Generator v2

## Purpose

WebStudio Generator v2 turns the local template-generator skill into a structured production system for simulated and future approved client landing page packages.

## Pipeline

1. Intake parsing: audience, offer, conversion goal, tone, assets, constraints, owner dependencies, acceptance threshold.
2. Industry risk classification: low, medium, regulated, medical, financial, data_sensitive.
3. Forbidden claims: guaranteed returns, medical outcomes, fake testimonials, unapproved integrations, fixed timelines/prices without assumptions.
4. Proof module selection: testimonial, credential, case study, process, third-party, or no-proof fallback.
5. Section architecture: hero, problem, mechanism, offer, proof, process, FAQ, dependency note, final CTA.
6. CTA strategy: consultation, booking, quote request, waitlist, lead magnet, or client-owned CRM placeholder.
7. SEO brief: title, meta, primary topic, secondary topics, terms to avoid, schema notes.
8. Responsive layout rules: mobile-first, semantic landmarks, accessible labels, contrast, reduced-motion tolerance.
9. QA handoff: static checks, accessibility hints, content proofing, compliance review, owner dependencies.
10. Delivery packaging: `/output` and `/workspace/templates` only unless separately approved.

## Safety

No real client changes, source changes, workflow changes, runtime/VPS/Supabase changes, deployments, or secrets are part of this system.
